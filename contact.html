FAQ

Common questions.

What kinds of clients does PrimeStrategy work with? +

We work with enterprises, financial institutions, government commissions, and non-profit organizations across Ghana and West Africa. Our clients tend to be organizations with a decision that requires defensible, primary-sourced data behind it — market entry, growth strategy, product launch, or regulatory response.

How long does a typical engagement take? +

Most consulting engagements run four to sixteen weeks depending on scope. Research programs — consumer studies, mystery shopping, market sizing — typically run three to eight weeks from kickoff to delivered report, factoring in field time.

Do you work outside Ghana? +

Yes. We have a liaison presence in Washington D.C. and run field programs across West Africa. For international clients entering the Ghanaian market, we frequently serve as the local research and advisory partner.

Can you build a custom research program for our company? +

That is most of what we do. Every program is scoped to the specific question our client is trying to answer — mix of quantitative surveys, qualitative interviews, focus groups, field audits, or analytics work. Custom scoping usually happens in the first two weeks.

How quickly will I hear back after submitting the form? +

Within two working days. Tender submissions and time-sensitive briefs are typically acknowledged the same day. If you have a deadline driving your enquiry, mention it in the message and we will prioritise accordingly.
